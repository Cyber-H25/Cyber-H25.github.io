Privacy Policy

Fall of Emperors: Iron & Might · Last updated November 3, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Fall of Emperors: Iron & Might ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by Hassan Darwish ("we", "us"), based in the United States.

In short: the game has no accounts and asks for no name, email or contacts. Your games, campaign progress and settings stay on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects device and usage information to show and measure ads. Online play uses Epic Online Services with an anonymous player ID. Buying Pro removes the ads.

Information that stays on your device

Saved games, campaign and Conquest progress, maps you create, your settings, the player name you type in Options, and whether you own Fall of Emperors Pro are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. Deleting the game deletes them.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows a banner on the main menu and optional rewarded videos (watched to try a locked civilization for 24 hours). Ads never appear during a match. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking) and an identifier for the app on your device
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • advertising and usage data, such as which ads were shown and tapped
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information about the ad SDK

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the game asks for your consent before showing ads, and you can change it any time in Options → Ad Privacy Choices. On iOS you can allow or refuse tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; if you refuse, ads are not personalized using the advertising identifier. Buying Fall of Emperors Pro removes all ads, and the ad SDK is then not started.

Online multiplayer

Online play uses Epic Online Services (Epic Games, Inc.). When you open the Online tab, Epic creates an anonymous ID tied to your device (no Epic account, email or name is needed) so you can host, find and join games. Your device's IP address is used to connect you to the other players, directly or through Epic's relay servers. The name of a game you host and the player name you choose are shown to other players in that game. See the Epic Games Privacy Policy. Playing only on your local Wi-Fi network connects devices directly and sends nothing to Epic.

Purchases

Fall of Emperors Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The game only records on your device that Pro is owned, and Restore Purchases asks Apple to confirm it.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports, which we use only to fix problems.

What we do not do

  • We do not run our own servers that collect your data, and we do not sell personal information.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA) you may have the right to access or delete personal information and to object to its use. Because we do not hold your data ourselves, most requests are handled by changing your ad choices above, deleting the game, or contacting Google or Epic through their privacy pages. You can also write to us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy as the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
