Common questions

How do I get my Pro purchase back on a new device?
Open Options → Restore Purchases (or the Unlock Pro screen → Restore Purchases) while signed in to the same Apple Account.

What does Pro include?
Every civilization, with their campaigns and Conquest empires, and no ads. The Romans, Gauls and Persians are free.

How do I try a locked civilization?
On the Unlock Pro screen, watch two short videos to play that civilization for 24 hours. One trial runs at a time, and the same civilization can be tried again after 7 days.

Online play says it could not start or sign in.
Check your internet connection and tap Refresh. Online play needs no account. If it keeps failing, write to us with a screenshot of the message.

Can I play with friends nearby?
Yes: Multiplayer → Local Network works on the same Wi-Fi without the internet. Allow "Local Network" access when iOS asks.

How do I change ad privacy choices?
Options → Ad Privacy Choices (shown where the law requires it), and iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Where are my saved games?
On your device. Deleting the game deletes them.
